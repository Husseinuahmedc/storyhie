# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and browser Canvas as specified by the project brief.

## Users
People who want to turn a personal photo into an Instagram story with a song attached to the moment.

## Product Purpose
أغنية لهاللقطة lets a user import a photo, choose or enter song details, compose a 9:16 story, and download a real PNG locally.

## Positioning
The product connects a specific photo and a song through a short English-language workflow that does not require an account or database.

## Operating Context
Mobile-first browser use, with the user’s photo and draft staying in the browser during the editing session. Spotify is an optional link preview.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Manual song entry must always work. Nine layouts, bilingual font choices, color palettes, crop positioning, captions, URL validation, and 1080 × 1920 PNG export are required. Only the search term or a validated Spotify track URL reaches a catalog endpoint.

## Brand Commitments
Sitehie typography, warm paper surfaces, dark ink, green accents, and the viewfinder music mark. Story text supports Arabic and English.

## Evidence on Hand
DESIGN.md, IMPLEMENTATION.md, references/*.webp, and assets/mark.svg. No production photo or licensed song artwork is supplied.

## Product Principles
- Keep the moment personal and local.
- Make the manual path complete.
- Show the composition before export.
- Preserve rights boundaries around provider artwork.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Controls need visible focus, accessible labels, keyboard reachability, and usable layouts at 320px and 390px CSS widths.
